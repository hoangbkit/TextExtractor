Café Signs & City Walks

On the walk toward the café, Renée noticed a faded sign that read “Bread & Coffee.” It was easy to miss from the main road, but the painted letters told a familiar neighborhood story.

Inside, the owner described how the shop had changed over twenty years — new neighbors, new recipes, and the same hand-written menu. Visitors still stopped to ask for directions before ordering a drink.

The street was quiet by late afternoon. One small doorway, a bicycle, and a row of windows helped make an ordinary block feel memorable for anyone willing to look closely.

A Small Garden for Pollinators

Even a narrow balcony can become a place for pollinators when plants bloom at different times of the year. A sunny corner, a few containers, and a little patience are enough to begin.

Choose flowers with different shapes so bees and butterflies have options across the season. Keeping a shallow dish of clean water nearby makes the space more welcoming through warm afternoons.

Skip decorative pesticides and leave some stems standing until spring. Small changes repeated across many homes can form a network of resting places for visiting insects.

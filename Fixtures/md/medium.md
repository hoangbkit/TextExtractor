# The Art of Brewing Coffee

Brewing an exceptional cup of coffee is both a **science** and an **art**. The process begins with selecting high-quality beans, which are typically sourced from regions near the equator. These beans are then roasted to perfection, bringing out their unique flavor profiles.

## Key Factors

- **Water temperature** should be between 195 and 205 degrees Fahrenheit
- **Grind size** must match the brewing method being used
- **Brewing time** varies from two to six minutes depending on the technique

> "Coffee is a language in itself." — Jackie Chan

The pour-over method, invented in Germany, remains a favorite among enthusiasts for its clean and vibrant results. A well-executed cup should have a balanced acidity, a pleasant sweetness, and a satisfying finish.

# The World of Honey

Honey is one of nature's most remarkable substances, produced by *Apis mellifera*, the western honey bee. A single bee colony can visit millions of flowers to produce just one kilogram of honey.

## Types of Honey

- **Manuka honey**: Known for its antibacterial properties
- **Acacia honey**: Light and mild, with a high fructose content
- **Wildflower honey**: Varies in flavor depending on the region and season

> "The honey bee is a wonderful creature. It works tirelessly, yet asks for nothing but the freedom to do its work." — Unknown

The process of honey-making begins when foraging bees collect nectar and store it in their honey stomachs. Enzymes in the stomach break down the complex sugars into simpler forms, and the bees then deposit the nectar into honeycomb cells where it is fanned dry by their wings.
